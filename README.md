# Superstore_Sales_Performance_Dashboard
Interactive Excel dashboard analyzing Superstore sales with PivotTables, Charts &amp; Slicers

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel | PivotTables | PivotCharts | Slicers  
**Program:** Data Analytics - Excel Module @ ExcelR, Hitech City, Hyderabad  
**Role:** Analyst

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes 4 years of Superstore sales data from 2014 to 2018. 
The objective was to identify revenue drivers, product performance, customer behavior, and regional trends to support business decisions.

The entire dashboard was built in Excel using PivotTables and interactive filters. It’s designed to give a business owner a quick view of "what’s selling, where, to whom, and how."

## 📊 Dashboard Walkthrough & Insights

### 1. Overall Sales Trend
The dashboard opens with `Sales by Year` and `Sales by Months`. 
This shows the company’s growth trajectory over 4 years. Monthly data helps spot seasonality and peak periods. 
The `Orders by Year` slicer at the top lets users filter the entire dashboard to compare a single year vs all years.

**Insight**: Sales have an upward trend year-over-year, which indicates business growth.

### 2. Product Analysis
`Sales by Top 5 Products` highlights the products that generate the most revenue — Chairs, Phones, Storage, etc.  
`Sales by Least Selling Products` shows the opposite: products like Labels and Envelopes that contribute very little.

**Insight**: The business is heavily dependent on a few key products. There’s an opportunity to either promote low sellers or discontinue them to reduce inventory cost.

### 3. Customer & Geographic Breakdown
`Sales by Top 10 Customers` shows customer concentration. A small group of customers drives a large portion of revenue.  
`Sales by Top 10 States` uses a donut chart to show which states bring in the most sales.  
`Sales by Region` treemap compares East, West, North, and South regions at a glance.

**Insight**: Revenue is not evenly distributed. Focusing marketing and retention efforts on top states/regions and top customers can improve ROI.

### 4. Operational Efficiency
`Total Sales by Shipment` breaks down how customers prefer to receive orders: Standard Class, Second Class, etc.

**Insight**: Most sales come through Standard Class. This can help the ops team forecast shipping costs and delivery timelines.
